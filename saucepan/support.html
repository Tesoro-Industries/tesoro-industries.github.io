Saucepan — Support

Your recipes in one calm place, with step-by-step cooking, timers, and a grocery list.

Getting help

Questions, bug reports, and feature requests: email dev@tesoroindustriesllc.com. Include your iOS version, the recipe link if it's an import problem, and what happened. Screenshots help.

Common questions

How do I add a recipe from a website? In Safari, tap Share, then Saucepan. Or tap + in the app and paste the link. You can check and edit everything before saving.

A recipe didn't import correctly. Some sites don't publish their recipe in a format apps can read. Tap "Paste recipe text instead" and paste the ingredients and steps; Saucepan sorts them out. Please email us the link so we can improve imports.

Do timers keep running if I leave the app? Yes. Allow notifications when asked and you'll get an alert when a timer is done.

Can I switch between US and metric? Yes. Tap the units chip next to Ingredients, or set it in Settings.

How do I change the app icon? Tap the gear on the main screen and pick an icon.

Is my data backed up? Recipes live on your iPhone and are included in your regular iPhone backups. See the privacy policy.
