Saucepan — Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Summary

Saucepan collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your recipes, photos, notes, grocery list, timers, cooking history, and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Links you share to Saucepan from other apps are passed to the app on the same device. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Network requests

  • Recipe websites. When you import a recipe, the app downloads that web page and its photo directly from the site you chose. That site sees your IP address, as it would if you opened the page in Safari. Nothing else about you is sent.
  • Timer alerts. Cooking timer notifications are scheduled on your iPhone. They are never sent to a server.

Tesoro Industries runs no servers for this app and never receives any of this information.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
