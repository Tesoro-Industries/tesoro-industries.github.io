Your next Caltrain, live times, the full schedule, and the weather on your commute.

Getting help

Questions, bug reports, and feature requests: email dev@tesoroindustriesllc.com. Include your iOS version, your stations, and what happened. Screenshots help.

Common questions

How do I set up my commute? Tap "Set up my commute" on the first screen, or go to More → Commutes. Pick your two stations, the days you ride, and when your return trip starts. You can save more than one commute and give each a color.

Why does it say "Scheduled" instead of live? When live predictions aren't available, the app shows Caltrain's timetable so you always see a train. Pull down to refresh.

What does "Live times are maxed out" mean? Live data comes from 511.org, which limits how often one key can ask for updates. Everyone shares the app's built-in key. You can get your own free key at 511.org/open-data/token and paste it in More → Live data key.

How current is the map on the Trains tab? Trains move along the timetable between refreshes, using the delay from the last update. Pull down to get the latest.

Can I see what my commute looks like on another day? Yes. Tap the calendar button on the Commute tab and pick a day.

Does the app track my location? No. See the privacy policy.

Data sources

Live train data from 511.org. Timetable from Caltrain's published schedule. Weather from Apple Weather. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Independence

My Caltrain is an unofficial, independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Caltrain or the Peninsula Corridor Joint Powers Board. Always check station signs and Caltrain service alerts when it matters.
