Verso

Read everything.

A quiet, beautiful home for the stories you actually read — fanfiction, web serials, and books. Bring your own words and Verso turns them into a clean, considered reading experience, on the page or read aloud.

Read

Typography that takes long-form fiction seriously. Scroll or turn pages. Warm light, sepia, and true-dark themes. Your place is always saved.

Listen

Narration reads any story aloud with word-by-word highlighting, lock-screen controls, a sleep timer, and a cursor that remembers where you paused.

Import

Paste text, open .txt or DRM-free EPUB files, or import a public Archive of Our Own work by link — title, author, and every chapter come along.

Quiet

No ads. No accounts. No tracking. Your library lives on your device and nowhere else.
