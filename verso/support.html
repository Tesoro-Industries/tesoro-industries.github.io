Verso — Support

A quiet, beautiful reader for fanfiction, web serials, and books.

Getting help

Questions, bug reports, and feature requests: email dev@tesoroindustriesllc.com. Include your iOS version and a description of what happened — screenshots help.

Common questions

How do I add a story? Tap + in the library. Paste text, open a .txt or DRM-free EPUB file, or paste a link to a public Archive of Our Own work.

Why can't Verso import a work? Works restricted to logged-in Archive users can't be fetched. EPUBs with DRM can't be opened.

How do I get better narration voices? iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices. Download any Premium or Enhanced voice and it appears in Verso automatically.

Where is my library stored? Entirely on your device. See the privacy policy.

Independence
