Verso — Content & Age Rating

Last updated August 26, 2026

What the app contains

Verso ships with a single sample story, suitable for all ages, so the app is usable the moment it opens. It bundles no other stories and hosts no content of its own.

Where everything else comes from

Every other story in a Verso library is fan fiction that the reader chose to bring in — by supplying a link, pasting text, or opening a file. Content imported by link comes directly from the third-party source site the reader points Verso at (for example, Archive of Our Own or Quotev) and remains subject to that site's own terms and ratings. Imported stories are stored only on the reader's own device.

What Verso does not do

Verso does not host, curate, recommend, rank, distribute, or generate any story. There is no feed, no catalog, and no sharing between users — nothing one reader imports is ever visible to another. Verso is a personal reading tool, closer to a browser's offline reading list than to a content service.

Why the age rating is set where it is

The app itself displays no mature material. Because a reader can choose to import fan fiction from source sites whose material may be mature, Verso's App Store age rating is set to reflect what a reader might bring in from those third-party sources — not content the app provides, contains, or shows on its own.
