Verso — Content & Age Rating

Last updated August 26, 2026

What the app contains

Verso ships one all-ages sample story. It bundles no other content and hosts nothing.

Where everything else comes from

Anything else in a library is material the user chose to import, at their own discretion — by link, pasted text, or file. Linked imports come directly from the third-party site the user selects (for example, Archive of Our Own or Quotev), under that site's terms, and are stored only on the user's device.

What Verso doesn't do

No hosting, curating, recommending, or distributing. No feed, no catalog, no sharing between users.

Why the age rating is set where it is

The app shows no mature material itself. The rating reflects what a user could import from those third-party sources — not content Verso provides.
