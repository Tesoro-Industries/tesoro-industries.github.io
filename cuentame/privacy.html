Cuéntame — Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Summary

Cuéntame collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your saved phrases, folders, practice history, recent translations, and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes all of it. If you export a backup file, it goes wherever you choose to save it.

Translation, voice, and the microphone

  • Translation. Cuéntame uses Apple's built-in translation. After the one-time Spanish download from Apple, translation happens on your iPhone.
  • Speaking. The microphone is used only while you tap a mic button. Your speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition. On supported iPhones this happens on the device; otherwise Apple may process it, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Listening. Spanish audio is spoken by your iPhone's built-in voices.

Tesoro Industries runs no servers for this app and never receives any of this information.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
