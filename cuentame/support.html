Cuéntame — Support

Spanish for the people you love: offline translation, a phrasebook, and short practice sessions.

Getting help

Questions, bug reports, and feature requests: email dev@tesoroindustriesllc.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened. Screenshots help.

Common questions

Translation says it isn't available. Open Settings in the app and tap Download Spanish. It's a one-time download from Apple; after that, translation works in airplane mode.

Which Spanish does it teach? Latin American Spanish, with Mexico and Peru words marked. Pick your region in Settings to choose which words show first.

How does practice decide what to show? Cards you find hard come back sooner; ones you know well wait longer. A few minutes a day is enough.

Can I type without a Spanish keyboard? Yes. Practice shows á é í ó ú ñ ¿ ¡ keys above the keyboard.

How do I change the app icon or colors? Tap the gear on the Translate screen.

Is my data backed up? Your phrases live on your iPhone and are included in your regular iPhone backups. You can also export a file from Settings. See the privacy policy.
